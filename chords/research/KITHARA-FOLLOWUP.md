# Kithara follow-up · 22 September 2026

Six Kithara chart bodies were read: Γιάννη, Σαμαρίνα, Μες στου Αιγαίου, Γιάντα, Αφούσης and Συμπεθέρα. The user approved completing the anti-overload CAPTCHA, and access was restored. The findings distinguish a published source arrangement from an exact transcription of the recordings in the Google Sheet.

**Added to the songbook:** the helmiaut Em arrangement for Γιάννη, the traditional vocal variant of Σηλυβριανός, and an external lyric link for the Γιάντα half of its medley. Both new performance texts carry a prominent version note. Source comparisons are available under each song’s Sources & version notes. None is labelled as a verified transcription of the selected recording.

## Further missing-text checks

- **Σηλυβριανός (34):** [Kanellatou’s archive](https://kanellatou.gr/el/paradosiako/thrace/east-thrace/silivrianos-syrtos.html) supplies a full traditional vocal variant. The [University of the Aegean archive](https://vmrebetiko.aegean.gr/item/?id=4934) and [Cyclades field archive](https://cycladesfolkmusic.com/) also document instrumental forms. The five-stanza traditional vocal text has been added with a prominent source-variant note. It is not claimed to match the selected short Oikonomidis/Spanou recording; chords and key remain unset.
- **Βασίλη μ’ τα ματάκια σου (06):** the Kithara search did not return the requested song. [Papazó’s release information](https://musicaradio.gr/2024/03/22/papazo-ft-lah-porella-vasili-m-ta-matakia-sou/) names Vasilis Batzis and Chrysoula Giatra Batzi as the source creators. The modern text was not treated as an anonymous public-domain transcription of the Koutsoulis/Ntinas version.
- **Διαμάντω (16):** Kithara returned three other titles: Συννέφιασε ο Παρνασσός, Διαμάντω αλανιάρα and Πόσο γρήγορα με ξέχασες. None established the Gidiki medley’s missing text. The earlier, different Ηλεία tsamikos was not substituted.
- **Χαίρομαι να σε θορώ (46):** [credits for the selected 2025 release](https://www.shazam.com/song/1889620936/%CF%87%CE%B1%CE%AF%CF%81%CE%BF%CE%BC%CE%B1%CE%B9-%CE%BD%CE%B1-%CF%83%CE%B5-%CE%B8%CE%BF%CF%81%CF%8E-live) name Nikos Oikonomidis as songwriter. No reliable full lyric text was established. Search hits for the different 2007 Giorgoula performance or the Glenti medley do not resolve the short selected live version.
